Assets Required
To make everything work correctly, the new email templates look for a set of 3D illustration assets under the /email/ path on your frontend domain.

You need to add the following image assets to your frontend's public/email/ folder:

Hero Illustrations (e.g., 3D Isometric styles like the reference):

hero-verification.png (OTP/Verification)
hero-success.png (Welcome/Approval/Success)
hero-security.png (Rejections/Alerts)
hero-payment.png (Receipts/Subscriptions)
hero-payment-failed.png (Failed Payments)
hero-invite.png (Invites & Admin Requests)
hero-reset.png (Password Resets)
Social Icons (to be placed in public/email/icons/):

twitter.png
facebook.png
instagram.png
